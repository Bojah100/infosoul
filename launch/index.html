Send them all at once, with the booking sheet attached

A web page is not allowed to send mail or attach files, so bulk sending runs on your own computer, through your own mailbox. It attaches the booking sheet to every email, sends one every 45 seconds, never sends the same one twice, and copies each to your mailbox. Open a terminal in the launch-deck folder and run these in order:

shows the plan; sends nothing
sends the first one to you only
asks you to type SEND, then your mailbox password

When it finishes, press Load the sender's record and choose launch-deck-tracking.json from that folder: every email it sent is marked as sent here. Text you edited on this page is not used by the bulk sender; it sends the queue as built.
